Welcome to the wiki for Fixer Simulator, an open source web game which is currently in active development!

I'm the lead developer and the creator of this wiki. Since the game isn't being promoted yet, there’s no community around it so far. Naturally, this wiki is still a work in progress, as my main focus is on the game itself - you can't document what hasn't been built yet, after all!

Eventually, the wiki will be directly integrated with the game’s features, so players won't need to waste time searching online for mechanics, guides, and more.

# Features

## Characters 
*Main article: [[Character]]*

**Characters** play a significant role in the game. Each individual in your world is unique, possessing a variety of attributes, occupations, and connections with others.
